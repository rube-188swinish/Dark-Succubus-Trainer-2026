## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Dark Succubus**. This trainer provides configurable assistance for labyrinth exploration, shard collection, succubus evasion, shop progression, and difficulty management.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the labyrinth at their own pace, reduce pursuit pressure, collect shards efficiently, experiment with shop purchases, or replay the story without repeated capture interruptions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement speed option carefully in narrow corridors and around hazards. A moderate multiplier is recommended for normal exploration, while higher values are better suited to open sections of the labyrinth. Disable the option before entering precision platforming areas if movement becomes difficult to control.

### Tools & Inventory

Dark Succubus uses points and a shop-based progression system rather than a traditional RPG inventory. Enable Unlimited Points to purchase available assistance items from the shop, then disable it before normal progression if you want to preserve the intended economy.

### World & Environment

The main objective is to navigate the dark labyrinth, collect soul or crystal shards, uncover story information, and find a route to escape. Succubi patrol the environment and can be avoided by changing direction, keeping distance, or using designated dodge points. Freeze Succubus and Shard Collection Assist are best used when learning layouts or revisiting completed areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Dark Succubus does not use conventional weapon-based combat. The central challenge is evasion: avoid the pursuing succubus, use the terrain, reach dodge points, and prevent repeated captures. God Mode and No Capture Count support relaxed exploration without changing the game's focus on pursuit and escape.
